Lab report 4: Efficiency of a simple Rankine cycle

{{trace.ta}}

The Rankine cycle is the idealised thermodynamic cycle of a steam power plant. It consists of four processes: isentropic compression in the pump, constant-pressure heat addition in the boiler, isentropic expansion in the turbine and constant-pressure heat rejection in the condenser. Raising the boiler pressure increases the mean temperature of heat addition, which raises the thermal efficiency of the cycle, although it also increases the moisture content at the turbine exit.

Privacy first

Built to guide students, not to watch them.

What we do

  • Teachers see summaries by defaultClass patterns first. Individual detail only when a teacher opens it.
  • Students see all their own dataEvery chat, score and replay about them is visible to them too.
  • Monitor only during announced examsLive Lab Mode runs for the session students were told about, then stops.

What we never do

  • Train AI models on student dataStudent work and conversations are never used to train any model.
  • Label anyone as dishonestWe show neutral evidence. Judgement stays with the teacher.
  • Monitor outside an exam sessionNo screen tracking during homework, lectures or personal time.
